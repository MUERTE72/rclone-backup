Privacy policy – rclone backup

This is a private, single-user application. It accesses Google Drive only on behalf of its owner, to upload and manage the owner's own backup files.

  • No data is collected from, or shared with, any third party.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own server.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking are used.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
